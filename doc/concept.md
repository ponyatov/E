# [[Evento]] naming concept

**Evento (E)** is a fantastic name—short, memorable, and clearly tied to **event-driven programming** for distributed embedded systems. Here’s why it works well:

### **Why "Evento (E)" Fits Perfectly**

✅ **Short & Easy** – "E" is minimal (great for constrained systems!).  
✅ **Event-Driven** – Matches the paradigm for distributed MCUs.  
✅ **Embedded-Friendly** – Sounds lightweight, like "Espresso" for code.  
✅ **Distributed Vibe** – Feels like events propagating across devices.

### **If You Want a Tagline:**

- _"Evento: Tiny Events, Big Distributed Systems."_
    
- _"E: Where Microcontrollers Communicate asynchronously."_
