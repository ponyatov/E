# Purpose

> Brief description of the [[E/E|E]] language's goals and target domain

The **purpose** of the [[E/E|E]] programming language prototype is to provide a specialized, high-level language for **embedded systems and industrial automation**, with a strong focus on **cross-platform compatibility, concurrency, and safety**.

During the development of the language, the special attention was paid to the possibility and ease of E language use by domain specialists without professional programming skills.
