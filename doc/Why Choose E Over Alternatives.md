# Why Choose E Over Alternatives?

## Key Advantages of E Over Specific Alternatives

#### **1. vs. C/C++**

✅ **Memory-safe by default** (no segfaults, buffer overflows)  
✅ **Modern syntax** (pattern matching, algebraic types)  
✅ **Built-in concurrency** (no manual threads, sync and quirks)

#### **2. vs. Rust**

✅ **Easier for embedded** (less complexity for non-programmers)
✅ **REPL for live debugging** (Rust lacks interactive development)  
✅ **Lighter runtime** (no Rust’s stdlib overhead)

#### **3. vs. MicroPython/Lua**

✅ **Hard real-time** (no deep GC pauses, isolated per-thread GC)
✅ **Statically typed** (catches errors at compile-time)  
✅ **Faster execution** (compiled, not interpreted)

#### **4. vs. Elixir**

✅ **Runs on microcontrollers** (Elixir needs BEAM VM and huge RAM)  
✅ **Manual memory control** (critical for RTOS)  
✅ **No VM overhead** (direct bare-metal compilation)

#### **5. vs. Espruino**

✅ **Not JavaScript** (Espruino’s JS quirks hurt embedded use)  
✅ **Statically typed** (Espruino is dynamic)  
✅ **Better performance** (native compiled vs. interpreted JS)
✅ **Optimized for production use** (hardware I/O & DMA, not a toy language)
